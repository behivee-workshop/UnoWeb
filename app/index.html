Keybench three small MIDI apps, one shared keyboard and device connection
Live

Play and watch — chord detection, scale/arpeggio recognition, and a glowing note chart.

 Sheet

A 4-track step sequencer. Each track keeps its own 1-4 bar length, shown one bar at a time.

 Looper

A Boss RC-5-style live looper for MIDI — per-track play/pause, overdub, and clear.
